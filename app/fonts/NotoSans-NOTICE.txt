NotoSans-Subtitle-Medium.woff2 is a static instance of Noto Sans at
wght=500, wdth=95, generated with fontTools.varLib.instancer and encoded
as WOFF2. All original glyphs and copyright/license metadata are retained.
The static instance supports Tizen versions without variable-font support
and is bundled locally so playback does not require a font download.

Selected as a visual approximation of NRK Sans after comparing Noto Sans,
Open Sans and Source Sans 3. Background and spacing follow the NRK TV
web-player styling referenced in css/style.css.
